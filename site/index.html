VI

Questions

How much space do you need?

About 10 by 12 feet and one outlet. A conference room, lobby corner or classroom is plenty.

Do you come to our house?

Yes. Most family and holiday sessions happen at home. We bring the backdrop, lights and screen and pack it all up when we're done.

When do we get the photos?

You see the shots live, then the edited photos arrive in a private online gallery. Holiday sessions are delivered in time for cards and gifts.

Do you travel?

We're based in Los Angeles and serve Carson, Compton, Long Beach, Torrance, Inglewood and all of LA. For the right shoot we travel anywhere in California.

How many people can you photograph?

From one person to a full family reunion or a whole office. Tell us your headcount and we'll plan the time.

Do you do anything besides photos?

We also do DJ, sound, lighting and live streaming through our sister company, Controlling All Music.
